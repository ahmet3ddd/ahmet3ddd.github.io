Learns by making the things he is curious about.

Designs, codes and makes. His work moves between space and visualisation, small software tools that run in the browser, machines and objects built in his own workshop, and a study of how knowledge is carried from one generation to the next. Research

An interactive, bilingual (TR / EN) study.

Aktarım Zinciri / The Transmission ChainHow is knowledge passed down the generations, and where does it break? The study models transmission as a chain of six links and tests the model on 32 real objects, without treating writing and language as special. Assessments in the study were made with AI models; it is not peer-reviewed. Open source, MIT licence.

Design

Architectural and interior projects and 3D visualisation.

He ran 3D Dizayn from 1994 until it closed in early 2026: architectural and interior projects, site work, industrial product design, 2D/3D animation and perspectives, for the company's own projects and for 21 architecture and construction firms. 62 furniture, interior and architectural detail models made in 3ds Max and V-Ray are on CGTrader; some of them are the work of his 3D Dizayn partner Mim. Şenay Gök Ataman. He studied interior architecture at Marmara University, then photography and media at Anadolu University.
